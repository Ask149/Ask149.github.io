A. The Notebook Shelf

A wooden shelf at top with four book spines — each a different cover. Open book = current persona. Click a spine → that notebook opens, the page transforms to its visual world. Persistent across all rooms. Build: medium. Extends the R metaphor. Most cohesive with what is already designed.

B. The Cartographer’s Map

A hand-drawn aerial map at top. Four locations pinned (workshop · road · cabinet · bench). Click a pin → camera flies/zooms to that location; the room beneath shifts to its world. Map shrinks to a compass-corner minimap inside each room. Build: high. Most spatial. Best for "exploring my life" feeling.

C. The Horizontal Panorama

One long horizontal canvas. Scroll right (or swipe) to walk from Workshop → Road → Cabinet → Bench. Each scene is its own visual world; transitions blend at the edges. Indicator dots show position. Build: very high. Most cinematic. Mobile UX needs care (vertical fallback). Highest "creative + artistic" score.
