Which personas are real for you?

Multi-select. Pick the ones that are genuinely YOU — the ones where you have material to show or could plausibly maintain. Don’t pick aspirational ones; we’d ship empty rooms. Add missing ones in the terminal.

§1

The Coder / AI Builder Default · Locked

The manuscript notebook (R direction). Microsoft, OpenCode, 4 agents in parallel, live feed, /changelog, manifesto, technical writing. The professional first page.

§2

The Traveler

Places visited, photos, location stamps, travel notes. Could feel like a passport, postcards, an atlas, or a stamped customs page. Visual-heavy — needs real photos to land.

§3

The Curator (Fragrance / Music / Fashion / Books)

Taste as identity. Fragrance wardrobe, current playlists, books finished this year, wardrobe pieces, notes on craft. Could feel like a cabinet of curiosities, a museum vitrine, or a reading-room.

§4

The Reader & Writer

Long-form essays beyond tech, book reviews, margins of life, philosophical writing. Continuation of the manuscript voice but for non-technical work.

§5

The Personal Life (Wedding / Relocation / Family)

The Jun 27 wedding, the Seattle→Pune move, family stories, the human behind the engineer. Most intimate. Probably guarded — small audience, soft typography.

§6

The Maker / Tinkerer (IRL)

Physical things — sketches, diagrams, hardware, electronics, drawings, photos of things made. Distinct from coding. Visual-heavy. Skip if you don’t actually make physical things.

§7

The Thinker (Philosophy / Long-form)

Deep dives, philosophy of work, philosophy of AI, taste, life choices. Overlaps with Reader; pick only if there’s a distinct “essayist” voice separate from your tech writing.

Guideline

Architecturally, 3–5 personas is the sweet spot. More than that scatters; fewer reads as “just a portfolio.” Each persona becomes a “room” (or “notebook,” or whatever metaphor we lock next) with its own background, typography accent, and design language — but knit together by a consistent navigation gesture.

Click any cards that apply, then in the terminal: tell me what I missed, OR confirm with the list, OR tell me “these three: coder, traveler, curator.” Once we know the cast, I can show 3–4 structural metaphor options for the navigation (notebook shelf / hand-drawn map / horizontal world / passport book / etc.).
