Seattle, summer of 2024

first ferry · jun
rainier · jul
pike place · sep
last view · may

Two years here. The rain stopped surprising me by month four. I learned to love the way the city held still under low clouds — ferries dissolving into Puget Sound, the Space Needle going quiet on grey afternoons. Mt Rainier appeared maybe twenty times the entire time, and every appearance felt like a small reward.
