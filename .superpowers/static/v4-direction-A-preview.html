§ 3 Travels · Filed in dispatches
Filed from Pune, India
No. 04 · June 2026 · 8 photos

Three days back in Pune at the tail end of the monsoon. The city I left in 2022 had changed in the small ways that matter — a new metro line, an espresso bar in the colony where there used to be only filter coffee, the same auto-rickshaw drivers somehow charging twice the fare. We drank cardamom chai in a café in Koregaon Park that wouldn't have existed when I was at PICT, and I thought about how a city can be both completely unchanged and totally different.

The light in Pune at the end of the monsoon is silver. Photographers say there are only two good hours in a day — the hour after sunrise and the hour before sunset — but the silver hour in Pune lasts until almost noon if the rain stops at the right time. I shot most of these on a Ricoh GR IIIx, which is small enough to carry without thinking and slow enough that you have to think before you press it.
